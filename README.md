## BRouter Endurance Profiles
A set of custom profiles for BRouter (compatible with mobile apps and web services like [bikerouter.de](https://bikerouter.de/) or [brouter.de](https://brouter.de/brouter-web/)).
These profiles are designed for fast, predominantly paved routes that still allow for occasional off-road shortcuts. If you prefer asphalt but don't mind including 0.5% to 20% of unpaved segments, these profiles are for you.
#### Best for:
* Weekend adventures.
* Long-distance touring.
* Brevets and endurance training.

Bike type: Endurance, All-road, or Gravel bikes.

## Key Features
These profiles are based on the Trekking-Fast-wet profile by [Poutnik](https://github.com/poutnikl/Brouter-profiles), which sits between "Trekking" and "Road bike" styles. These custom versions make routing even faster by minimizing unnecessary turns and avoiding excessive detours.

## Profile Range
The profiles range from "adventure-ready" to "near-roadbike" efficiency:

| Profile | Character | Approx. Tires* |
|---|---|---|
| Endurance-Adventure | Closest to original Trekking-Fast-wet. Gladly accepts unpaved roads. For those who love light adventures. | ~42 mm |
| Endurance-Explore | Faster and more road-oriented. Less gravel, but still ready for unpaved roads. Author's daily choice (38mm semi-slicks). | ~40 mm |
| Endurance | Prefers secondary roads; rarely chooses unpaved sections. | ~35 mm |
| Endurance-Direct | Prefers main roads and high-quality secondary asphalt. Avoids unpaved roads where possible. | ~32 mm |
| Endurance-Express | Essentially a road bike profile. Sticks to main roads, using off-road shortcuts only in exceptional cases. | ~30 mm |

*Tire widths are very approximate guidelines. You might prefer Endurance-Express on a 2.5" MTB or Endurance-Adventure on 28mm slicks - it all depends on your riding style, personal preferences and the road conditions in your area.
## Tips for Use

* Test on familiar tracks: Before planning a trip to an unknown area, test profiles on routes you know well. This will help you understand the logic behind each preset.
* Safety first: Always review your generated route before heading out and follow local traffic laws. The profiles (especially Express and Direct) may route you along busy roads. Safety and legal compliance are the rider's responsibility.
